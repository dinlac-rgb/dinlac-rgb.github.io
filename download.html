Quick installation guide

  1. Register on this page — choose Free Trial or Paid Subscription. Your key is shown here and emailed to you.
  2. Download BizPulse_Setup_v1.0.0.exe. In Edge choose Keep; when it runs, choose More info → Run anyway if SmartScreen appears.
  3. Open BizPulse. On first launch, enter your Registered Email and Licence Key, then press Activate.
  4. Bought a plan after your trial? Enter the new key in Settings → License & Device. Your companies and vouchers are kept.
  5. Create your company and start entering vouchers.
