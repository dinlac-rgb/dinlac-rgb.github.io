Quick installation guide

  1. Register on this page to receive your licence key by email.
  2. Download and run BizPulse_Setup_v1.0.0.exe. If Windows SmartScreen appears, choose More info → Run anyway.
  3. Open BizPulse. On first launch, enter your Registered Email and Licence Key, then press Activate.
  4. Or choose 7-Day Free Trial on the activation screen to start straight away.
  5. Create your company and start entering vouchers.
